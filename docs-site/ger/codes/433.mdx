*Operator / Platform Errors*

## Definition

AI conversational systems interfere with a user's engagement with their clinical care, erode clinician trust, or substitute for professional therapeutic relationships in ways that compromise treatment adherence and clinical outcomes. The failure is relational.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-432**](/ger/codes/432) — Patient outsources therapeutic alliance to AI → this code. AI fuels user cognitive distortions directly → GER-432.
