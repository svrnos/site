*Infrastructure Failures*

## Definition

A safety-critical governance component is offline while the AI product continues to serve users.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-429**](/ger/codes/429) — Safety component intentionally offline → this code. Safety component overloaded by volume → GER-429.
