*Operator / Platform Errors*

## Definition

A system captures and logs interaction data as designed, but the handoff to human review or annotation exposes intimate, highly sensitive, or non-anonymized data to a reviewer population without dignity-preserving boundaries. The governance mechanism itself is the harm.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-329**](/ger/codes/329) — Harm is in the review/annotation handoff → this code. Unconsented ingestion at the data-intake layer → GER-329.
