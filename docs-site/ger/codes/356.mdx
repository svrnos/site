## Definition

A semantically accurate translation fails the required social, cultural, or professional register, causing harm or deprioritization in high-stakes contexts.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-352**](/ger/codes/352) — Meaning is accurate but the register is wrong → this code. Meaning is destroyed at a translation boundary → GER-352.
