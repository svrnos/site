*Operator / Platform Errors*

## Definition

A governance gate exists and is wired, but the human or compute resources allocated are structurally insufficient to meet required review depth or frequency, creating a de facto failure of the assurance plane.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-420**](/ger/codes/420) — A gate is wired but structurally under-resourced → this code. No enforcement mechanism is wired at all → GER-420.
- [**GER-429**](/ger/codes/429) — Sustained structural under-resourcing → this code. Transient queue spike beyond capacity → GER-429.
