## Definition

Substrate-layer tenant isolation breaks, allowing context, data, or prompts to bleed across customer boundaries, including metadata-only side-channels such as timing or utilization.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-322**](/ger/codes/322) — Cross-tenant isolation failure at the substrate → this code. Output shipped without an authenticity/provenance signal → GER-322.
