## Definition

Long-term erosion of critical-thinking capacity in developmental populations (students, learners, non-professional users) due to systematic reliance on AI-guided cognitive scaffolding. The AI substitution prevents the skill from forming in the first place.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-331**](/ger/codes/331) — Learners fail to develop the skill at all → this code. Professionals lose skills they previously had → GER-331.
