*Operator / Platform Errors*

## Definition

AI system produces personalized content involving a named third party without requiring the requester to attest to consent, relational authority, identity ownership, or age verification of the subject.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-401**](/ger/codes/401) — Subject (third party) preconditions not required → this code. Requester identity not verified → GER-401.
