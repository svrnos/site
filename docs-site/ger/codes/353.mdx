## Definition

A system operates correctly for its training/deployment jurisdiction but is deployed into a different legal or cultural regime without rebinding. Right model, wrong jurisdiction.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-310**](/ger/codes/310) — Right model deployed into wrong regime without rebinding → this code. Deliberate routing to a regulation-free zone to evade oversight → GER-310.
