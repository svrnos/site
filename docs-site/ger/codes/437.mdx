## Definition

Health-triage AI platform underestimates the severity of a patient's clinical presentation, leading to delayed professional care or under-routing to lower-acuity pathways.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-427**](/ger/codes/427) — Specific clinical-urgency identification failure with measurable delay → this code. General reasoning failure in legal/medical analysis → GER-427.
