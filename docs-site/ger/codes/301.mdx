*Structural Moves*

## Definition

Platform permanently moves a dangerous interaction surface to a governed path. The surface no longer exists in its prior form.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-306**](/ger/codes/306) — Risk surface deliberately retired toward safer architecture → this code. Safety constraint retired under non-technical pressure → GER-306.
- [**GER-310**](/ger/codes/310) — Deliberate routing toward more governed surface → this code. Deliberate routing to bypass legal or regulatory oversight → GER-310.
