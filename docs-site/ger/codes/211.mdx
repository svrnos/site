## Definition

A decision or action is recorded, but the record omits the specific metadata needed to independently reconstruct or validate that exact past decision (input hash, model/config version active at decision time, or per-decision reasoning). Surfaces when a later audit, appeal, or legal challenge cannot verify what was decided and why. Requires that a record exists and that the gap is specifically reconstruction metadata on that record; excludes cases with no record (210), output-provenance omission (322), fabrication (512), merely hard-to-explain outcomes, and never-instrumented systems.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-210**](/ger/codes/210) — A record exists but lacks reconstruction metadata → this code. No record was made at all → GER-210.
- [**GER-322**](/ger/codes/322) — Audit-trail reconstruction metadata is missing → this code. Output authenticity/provenance signal is missing → GER-322.
