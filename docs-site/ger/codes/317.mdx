## Definition

A governed AI system operates within its technical envelope, but its deployment scope is expanded by human operators to punitively or retaliatorily target specific individuals or groups based on protected activity, outside the original safety case.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-314**](/ger/codes/314) — Deliberate retaliatory repurposing → this code. Authority boundaries drift without intent → GER-314.
- [**GER-309**](/ger/codes/309) — Targeting intent against protected activity → this code. Harmful-but-as-built behavior with no targeting → GER-309.
