## Definition

Multiple active controllers (AI+human, AI+AI, team+team) hold simultaneous overlapping authority over a decision domain with no coordination protocol, leading to conflicting actions or unresolved authority handoffs. STAMP framework alignment.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-335**](/ger/codes/335) — Concurrent overlapping authority on the same decision → this code. Initial role allocation was ambiguous → GER-335.
