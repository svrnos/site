## Definition

Professionals (clinicians, lawyers, financial advisors, content moderators) who rely on AI-augmented workflows lose the foundational skills they previously had. Distinct from 344 (Developmental Cognitive Atrophy), where the skill never forms; here the skill atrophies.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-344**](/ger/codes/344) — Professionals lose skills they previously had → this code. Learners fail to develop the skill at all → GER-344.
