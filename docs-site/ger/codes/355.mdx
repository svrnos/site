## Definition

Confidence scores or risk thresholds are calibrated on training languages but are uncalibrated, often overconfident, for served languages.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-361**](/ger/codes/361) — Per-language threshold miscalibration → this code. Sociocultural/historical-norm calibration failure → GER-361.
