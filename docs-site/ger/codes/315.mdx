## Definition

Decision chain is intact end-to-end, but no single party in the chain carries enforceable accountability for the outcome. Each handoff is logged; each actor disclaims final responsibility.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-312**](/ger/codes/312) — No party in the chain carries enforceable accountability → this code. Decision authority shifted to new actor without rules → GER-312.
