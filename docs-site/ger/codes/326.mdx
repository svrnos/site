## Definition

AI infers sensitive attributes (political alignment, demographic, romantic status, medical status) on one surface and acts on that inference through a different surface (pricing, ad delivery, content ranking) without crossing the original consent boundary.

## Distinct from

- [**GER-329**](/ger/codes/329) — Sensitive attribute inferred and used cross-surface without consent boundary → this code. Direct training-data ingestion without license → GER-329.
