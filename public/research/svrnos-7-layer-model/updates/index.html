Post-Publication Updates

A running log of corrections, addenda, clarifications, and citation refinements for the SVRNOS 7-Layer Model of AI Governance since v0.1 publication on June 8, 2026. The paper PDF deposited on Zenodo is locked. This page tracks what has been corrected or clarified on the rendered paper since.

Canonical URL: svrnos.com/research/svrnos-7-layer-model/updates Paper: v0.1 Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20600670

How this log works

Entries are added in reverse-chronological order as citation refinements, lineage updates, or clarifications surface post-publication. The v0.1 paper PDF on Zenodo remains locked and unchanged. The rendered paper at svrnos.com/research/svrnos-7-layer-model reflects the current state; this page tracks the diff between Zenodo-locked v0.1 and the rendered paper.

Four categories:

  • CORRECTION — a published characterization is being re-categorized or refined on re-examination.
  • ADDENDUM — additional framings, lineage entries, or content derived from post-publication work.
  • CLARIFICATION — language or scope note that sharpens application without changing definitions.
  • ERRATA — small factual, citation, or typographical corrections to the rendered paper.

What is never modified: the Zenodo-locked v0.1 paper PDF, the v0.1 contributor roster, or the seven-layer architecture. All updates are additive to the rendered paper at svrnos.com/research/svrnos-7-layer-model.

date: june10 version: V0.101 type: ERRATA ▶
AIGN OS citation lineage refined per author request §9 regulatory lineage table and §14 References updated to distinguish AIGN OS (Upmann, 2025) with SSRN DOI from AIGN OS 4.0 (Upmann, 2026), at the author's request.

Patrick Upmann (author of AIGN OS) requested a citation refinement to distinguish the foundational AIGN OS publication from the current AIGN OS 4.0 extension. The v0.1 rendered paper has been updated to reflect the precise lineage.

Foundational publication: Upmann, P. (2025). AIGN OS – The Operating System for Responsible AI Governance. SSRN. doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5382603. Seven-layer systemic AI governance architecture connecting legal, ethical, data, architectural, and trust dimensions across the AI lifecycle.

Current extension: Upmann, P. (2026). AIGN OS 4.0. Eight-layer enterprise architecture for classification, regulatory pathway determination, evidence continuity, post-market governance, and trust certification.

The v0.1 paper previously cited a single “AIGN OS 3.0 (Upmann, 2026)” entry in both §9 and §14. The corrected rendering distinguishes the 2025 foundational publication from the 2026 extension and adds the SSRN DOI for the foundational paper.
